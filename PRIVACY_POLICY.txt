- App Scope: A minimal iOS app that delivers one personalized business idea per day, with optional Pro features.

2) Information We Collect
A) Information you provide
- Persona preferences: industries of interest, budget range, and time availability (used to bias daily ideas).
- Saved items: ideas you save locally on your device (Pro feature).
- Purchase intent and paywall interactions (non-sensitive selections and UI events).
- Support communications if you contact us.

B) Information collected automatically
- Device and app metadata: app version, build number, OS version, country/locale, language, timestamps, and generic device model.
- Event telemetry: pseudonymous app usage events (e.g., idea viewed/saved/shared; onboarding completed; paywall shown/selected) to improve reliability and user experience.
- Push notification token and delivery status.

C) Information from third parties
- In‑app purchase and subscription status from Apple and our purchase processor (RevenueCat) to enable Pro features.
- Analytics events processed by TelemetryDeck (privacy‑respecting pseudonymous analytics).
- Push delivery and segmentation via OneSignal (using a pseudonymous external user ID and push token).

3) How We Use Your Information
- Deliver the daily business idea and personalize it to your stated preferences.
- Provide app functionality: notifications, widgets, saving/sharing/exporting, and Pro features.
- Maintain reliability and performance, prevent abuse, and diagnose problems.
- Process subscriptions, verify entitlements, and manage paywall experiences.
- Measure app usage (non‑sensitive analytics) to improve product quality and retention.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4) AI Content Generation
- The backend may use third‑party AI providers to generate idea content based on de‑identified persona preferences and category tags. We do not send your name, email, or precise identifiers for idea generation. Where available, we configure providers to minimize data retention and restrict use of prompts/responses for model training. We do not control third‑party providers’ internal policies; see their privacy notices for more details.

5) Data Storage and Processing
- On‑device data: Persona preferences and saved ideas are stored locally on your device. Removing the App deletes this local data. Backups depend on your device/iCloud settings.
- Backend infrastructure: Our server runs on Cloudflare Workers with Cloudflare D1 (database) and KV (cache/config). Requests for daily ideas may include your persona preferences so we can fetch or generate an appropriate idea. We store the ideas and limited request context necessary to operate the service and combat abuse. We avoid storing information in a form that directly identifies you (no account or profile with name/email).
- Notifications: OneSignal processes your push token and pseudonymous tags (e.g., categories of interest, free/pro status) to deliver notifications and measure delivery.
- Purchases: RevenueCat processes your purchase receipts and a pseudonymous user identifier to verify entitlements and manage subscriptions.
- Analytics: TelemetryDeck processes pseudonymous event telemetry (e.g., app version, feature usage). We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or address book data.
