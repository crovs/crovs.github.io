Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Who we are

Crovs Poster ("the Service") is an internal publishing tool operated by LUMI sp. z o.o. (KRS 0001208216, NIP 5253070074), Aleja "Solidarności" 68/121, 00-240 Warsaw, Poland ("we", "us"). Our team uses it to schedule and publish videos we produce ourselves to YouTube channels and Instagram accounts that we own and operate. It is not offered to the public; only members of our team can sign in.

Contact for privacy questions: privacy@hitsy.app

2. YouTube API Services

The Service uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions and publish times, read basic channel and video statistics, and reply to comments on our own channels. By connecting a YouTube channel to the Service you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information we access, collect and store

  • Google account authorization: when a channel owner connects a channel, we receive an OAuth refresh token for that channel. It is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and used only to act on that channel.
  • Channel data: channel ID, channel name and profile image, used to show which channel a post goes to.
  • Our own content: the video files, titles, descriptions and publish times we create for our posts.
  • Statistics and comments: public view, like and comment counts of our own videos, and comments on them, so we can track performance and reply.
  • Team sign-in: the work email addresses of team members, used to sign in through Cloudflare Access.

We do not collect data about viewers beyond the public comments and aggregate statistics YouTube shows to the channel owner. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How we use it

Only to publish our own videos at the scheduled time, show our team the status and performance of those posts, and reply to comments on our own channels.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored in Cloudflare (Workers and R2 storage). Authorization tokens are encrypted at rest, and the Service is protected by Cloudflare Access so only authorized team members can reach it.

6. Retention and deletion

Authorization tokens are kept while a channel is connected and deleted when it is disconnected. Post records and statistics are kept while the channel is managed by us. To request deletion of any stored data, email privacy@hitsy.app; we delete it within 7 days.

You can revoke the Service's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.

7. Cookies

The Service uses only the sign-in cookie set by Cloudflare Access. It uses no tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, or object to its processing, by emailing privacy@hitsy.app. You may also complain to the Polish data protection authority (UODO).

9. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the new date at the top.
